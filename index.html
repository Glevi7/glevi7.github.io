About

SYSTEMS
THINKER.

I'm Guy Levi, an Aerospace Engineering student at The Ohio State University with a passion for autonomous aircraft, flight controls, and aerospace systems. My experience spans systems engineering, avionics, simulation, ground control systems, and multidisciplinary aircraft development, giving me a broad understanding of how complex aerospace systems are designed, integrated, and tested.

I am driven by solving challenging engineering problems and enjoy developing reliable systems that bridge software and hardware. My goal is to contribute to the next generation of autonomous aircraft and advanced aerospace technologies in the defense and commercial flight industries.
